          A roleplay market. Hrossagrith is a fictional state and this is a game. No real horse, pony, donkey or mule is sold, loaned, reserved or delivered here, no money is taken, and every seller, brand, listing and price is invented.

          Prices are in crowns (cr), a provisional unit until the Empire’s currency is defined.

          Every listing follows the Equid Welfare Code: disclosure (Art. 91), suitability (Art. 92), record continuity (Art. 93) and companionship (Art. 39). Tack is listed only if it meets the Imperial Tack Measurement Schedule.
