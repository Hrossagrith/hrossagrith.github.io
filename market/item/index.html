                Horses Tack

                A roleplay market. Hrossagrith is a fictional state and this is a game. No real horse, pony, donkey or mule is sold, reserved or delivered here, no money is taken, and every seller, brand, listing and price is invented.

                Prices are in crowns (cr), a provisional unit until the Empire’s currency is defined.

                Every listing follows the Equid Welfare Code: disclosure (Art. 91), suitability (Art. 92) and record continuity (Art. 93). Tack is listed only if it meets the Imperial Tack Measurement Schedule.
