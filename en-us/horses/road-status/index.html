Book III · Standard

Public-road equid status marking

The final and binding universal standard for every public-road equid. The Carriage Code may impose additional or stricter requirements on top of it.

Every equid entering a public road shall wear approved public-road bitless headgear fitted with an approved Facial Marker displaying that equid’s current interchangeable retroreflective and switchably illuminated road-status insert. The marker is a road instruction to humans. It shall not be used as advertisement, as decoration alone, as medical disclosure beyond necessity, as shame marking, or as a replacement for the judgment of the rider or driver.

Definitions

Frentera and muserola

A frentera is a frontal component running vertically down the center of the face from the upper bridle, headstall, browband or crown area toward the forehead or nasal bridge. It may be narrow, X-shaped, branched, broad or panel-like. A component confined to crossing or encircling the nose, with no upper vertical facial connection, is not a frentera.

A muserola is a noseband or nosepiece crossing and ordinarily encircling the nose. It may be broad, decorative, separately mounted, or integrated into a lawful control noseband, and need not attach to the browband or crownpiece.

Either qualifies as a Facial Marker only where it provides a dedicated, clearly visible insert field of sufficient size and contrast for road use. A small ornament, medallion, stud or badge that cannot carry the insert visibly does not qualify.

No body marking No paint, dye, clipping, branding, glue, mane attachment, tail attachment, skin marking or coat alteration may be used to show status. Ever.

Ordinary and restricted markers

Color is never the only distinction

  • White field Ordinary road-certified equid.
  • Green field Green equid, provisional certificate, training status, or supervised road work.
  • Yellow field Caution: sensitivity, extra-space requirement, or heightened startle risk.
  • White field, red border Restricted passing. Pass only on the signal of the driver, rider or handler.
  • Red field, white X Do not pass. Stop and wait: an active hazard or immediate control condition exists.

Service markers

Issued and verified through authorized channels only

  • Blue, Road Guard emblem Imperial Road Guard, traffic patrol, road-control service.
  • Blue, police shield Police service, where authorized.
  • White, red cross Ambulance or authorized medical emergency service.
  • Red, white flame Fire or rescue service.
  • Green, paw and hoof Veterinary emergency service.
  • Blue, pegasus Imperial post, messenger or courier service.
  • Black, white border Sovereign passage, where legally authorized.
  • Black with crown Authorized imperial government, ministerial or state authority passage.
  • Dark green, sigil Military service.

The drawings are not finished. Everything set out on this page is in force: the fields, borders, emblems, symbols, contrast, retroreflective material, switchable illumination, night and weather visibility and color-blind accessibility are all binding, and an equid entering a public road is marked accordingly. What is outstanding is the graphical part of the standard. Official graphical drawings and approved-product illustrations remain to be made, and the archive records that the official marker graphics still need final design. The discs above show each status by field, border and symbol as the standard requires; they are not yet the approved final artwork.

Placement

Front, side and rear

  • Front: the interchangeable insert, displayed in a marker-bearing frentera or muserola.
  • Side: a round or approved status plate on harness, breastcollar, breastplate, saddlecloth or other non-restraining equipment.
  • Rear carriage board: markers arranged to match team formation, so road users can identify the status of each equid in a hitch.

A nose-mounted marker shall sit over the stable nasal bone, clear of lower nasal cartilage and nostrils. Where integrated into a pressure-bearing noseband, nothing about the window, backing, edging or fasteners may stiffen the noseband, create hard edges or pressure points, concentrate force, impair fit or release, alter rein or line action, or restrict breathing.

Minimum dimensions

Clear visible field, shortest dimension

  • 70 mm front insert for an equid above 300 kg
  • 60 mm for an equid of 100–300 kg
  • 50 mm for an equid below 100 kg
  • 90 mm side status plate
  • 120 mm rear carriage-board marker

Borders, symbols and divided fields must stay proportionate and may not reduce any required clear field below these minima.

Recognition test

Measured, not asserted

A front marker shall be correctly identified by a person with ordinary corrected vision at not less than 50 m in daylight and 30 m at night; side and rear markers at 60 m and 40 m. Night testing is passed both in powered illuminated mode and in unpowered retroreflective mode. Testing includes frontal and fifteen-degree offset views, walking and ordinary road movement, wet conditions, partial forelock movement, full and low battery states, and deliberate switching on and off.

Illumination

Identification lighting, not emergency lighting

Every approved marker operates both in an unpowered road-grade retroreflective mode and in an independently switchable, steady self-illuminated mode reproducing its assigned field color, border, shape and symbol. Illumination is required from sunset to sunrise, in tunnels, and whenever fog, rain, snow, smoke, dust or glare materially reduces recognition.

Illumination shall be steady. It shall not flash, strobe, pulse, rotate, sweep, alternate colors, imitate a turn, brake, hazard or emergency lamp, project a beam onto the road, or act as a ground-guidance lamp. Status-marker illumination is identification lighting and creates no emergency priority.

The emitter is diffused and shielded so that no direct light source is visible from either equid eye position. The complete electrical assembly is at least IP65 equivalent. A powered marker remains fully retroreflective when switched off or when power fails, and its battery reserve covers the planned journey plus not less than two hours.

Priority order

Where more than one status applies

  1. Active hazard or stop
  2. Restricted passing
  3. Authorized emergency service
  4. Police or Road Guard traffic control
  5. Pregnancy or medical vulnerability
  6. Yellow caution
  7. Green or provisional status
  8. Ordinary road certification

Secondary statuses appear on approved side or rear markers. A service status shall not erase or conceal pregnancy, green, caution, restricted, hazard or suspension status. Decorative combinations, blended colors, half-symbols and unofficial overlays are prohibited.

Offenses

What is punished

Displaying a false status; using an unreadable marker; concealing a required status; displaying a lower-priority status instead of a higher one; using an unauthorized service emblem; altering a marker to resemble another status; attaching a marker directly to the body; failing to activate required illumination; using flashing, pulsing, color-changing, dazzling or service-imitating illumination; or continuing road use after marker or power failure, except direct lawful movement to safety.

Unauthorized manufacture, sale, lending, display, imitation, alteration, duplication, possession for deceptive use, or failure to surrender a revoked service marker is likewise an offense. Ordinary non-service markers, by contrast, may be freely manufactured, stocked, sold and possessed: possession alone is not an offense, and the offense is false display or misrepresentation.

Availability. Official marker specifications, meanings, authorized emblems and public-recognition guides are publicly accessible. The physical official markers and credentials are manufactured, issued and supplied only through authorized channels to verified services.

Dark fields Black, very dark green and other dark service fields carry a continuous white or silver retroreflective outer border not less than one-tenth of the marker’s shortest dimension, plus a high-contrast emblem. A dark field without that border, emblem and lawful illumination capability is not road-valid at night.

Inspection Commercial, public, service, rental, freight, ceremonial, emergency and government markers get a recorded check before each working day and a formal inspection at least every six months.

If it fails Stop at the first safe place and clean, uncover, correct, recharge, repair or replace. A handwritten note, ribbon, body paint, phone screen or ordinary light is not a substitute.
