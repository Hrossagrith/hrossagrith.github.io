Work and money

Progressive taxation, serious treatment of avoidance, and one prohibition that surprises visitors.

Income, wealth, capital gains and large inheritances are progressively taxed. Aggressive tax avoidance and the hiding of wealth abroad are serious offenses rather than clever arrangements, and they are treated as such.

Artificial intelligence

A prohibition rather than a policy preference

AI data centers are prohibited within Hrossagrið. Foreign AI services may be accessed only under strict privacy, energy, copyright and national-security rules.

In preparation. Employment law, wages, pensions, industrial organization, currency and the imperial budget process are not yet written into the register. The constitutional continuity budget — what happens to public money when the two Crowns deadlock — is described under government.

Meritocracy One of the eight foundational values. Registers and professional rosters are maintained by permanent civil servants under published objective qualifications; applicants cannot be excluded for political loyalty or personal favor.
