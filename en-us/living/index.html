Book II

Living in Hrossagrið

What the Empire provides, requires and guarantees for the people who live within its protection.

The sections below follow from one principle rather than from a list of policies. The legitimate purpose of state power in Hrossagrið is to make existence as constructive and as little destructive as possible across the whole breadth of life, judged on the best available evidence about what a decision will actually do. Free healthcare, free education and strict child protection are not generosity: they are what that principle produces when it is applied honestly.

Where this comes from See the state and its doctrine for eudaimonocracy and the two Crowns.

The register of parts

Sections of this book

Six parts, each held separately in the register

  • Part I

    Health

    Free at the point of use, heavily funded, and designed against waiting lists, staff shortages and regional inequality.

  • Part II

    Education & children

    Free education at every level, free childcare regardless of parental employment, and exceptionally strong protection for children.

  • Part III

    Transport & roads

    Horse-drawn road transport, rail, and the single narrow motor-freight exception known as Hrossaway.

  • Part IV

    Justice & policing

    Police selection and training on an aviation model, de-escalation by default, and an absolute prohibition of the death penalty.

  • Part V

    Work & money

    Progressive taxation of income, wealth, capital gains and large inheritances.

  • Part VI

    Citizenship & refuge

    A diverse human nation descended from many peoples integrated over centuries through migration and refuge.
