Book III

The imperial legal corpus

Codes in force, the technical standards that carry their measurable detail, the official templates they create, and the law still reserved for drafting.

Because the state name itself remembers protected passage, equid welfare is not decorative symbolism. It sits near the center of imperial law. Protecting a person while allowing their horse or other equid to be abused, seized, overworked or used as leverage is not true protection, and every instrument below is read against that principle.

Reading order A code states obligations and offenses. A standard carries the measurable detail and is amended more often. A template is a form whose layout is fixed by the instrument that creates it.

Codes in force

Binding law

  • In force · General code

    General Imperial Equid Welfare Code

    The principal welfare instrument of the Empire, covering equids in every setting rather than one line of work.

  • In force · Sectoral code

    Imperial Code for the Welfare of Working Carriage Equids

    Carriage welfare in full: passports, certification, workload and rest, weather burden, route safety, harness, headgear mounts, road-status inserts, lighting, ground-guidance lamps, emergency medical carriages, enforcement, offenses and interim schedules.

  • In force · Sectoral code

    Imperial Tack, Restraint, and Welfare Code

    Equipment placed on the animal, and the limits on restraint. The Empire’s bitless-only welfare principle is stated here.

  • In force · Sectoral code

    Imperial Road and Driver Code

    Conduct on public roads and the duties of drivers, with the universal requirement that every equid entering a public road displays its road status. Its Chapter XI also fixes the national transport structure: the public road network is a non-motor road system, motor road vehicles are prohibited outside the narrow Hrossaway freight exception, mechanized public land transport is limited to long-distance high-speed electric mainline rail — no metro, underground, tramway, suburban commuter, light or local rapid-transit rail, and no diesel or steam service — and no level crossing, road-running rail or shared tramway surface is permitted.

  • In force · Sectoral code

    Imperial Mounted Service and Road Patrol Code

    Binding law for mounted police, the mounted Road Guard, ridden couriers and patrol equids — ridden service, which is not carriage work and is governed separately. Its status is mixed and the archive is not consistent about it: twenty-one articles on certification, command, selection, training, tack, shift limits, rest days, climate bands, traffic and crowd duties, emergency response, facilities, records, inspection, offenses and appeals are in force, while the same document separately lists ten chapters, from Application and Definitions to Enforcement, Records, Offenses and Appeals, as reserved for later drafting.

Technical standards

Thresholds, dimensions, colors and testing

  • In force · Standard

    Imperial Public-Road Equid Status Marking Standard

    The universal marking rule for every equid on a public road. Status is shown by an interchangeable insert held in a marker-bearing frentera or muserola, road-grade retroreflective and self-illuminating in its assigned color. No paint, dye, clipping, branding, glue or coat alteration is permitted.

  • In force · Standard

    Imperial Service Equid Selection and Breed Standard

    Selection for police, ambulance, taxi, courier, veterinary, fire, military and sovereign service. Endurance, recovery, road courage, temperament, traffic sanity and lawful bitless control decide fitness — not breed label or fashion. Individual certification overrides breed label.

  • In force · Standard

    Imperial Equine Climatic Burden Standard

    Weather, humidity, wetness, cold, heat, wind, footing and suspension levels, expressed as burden thresholds.

  • In force · Standard

    Imperial Carriage Lighting and Signaling Standard

    Carriage lamps, emergency warnings, illuminated road-status markers and equid ground-guidance lamps.

  • In force · Standard

    Imperial Emergency Medical Carriage Technical Standard

    Ambulance speed, low jostle, suspension, emergency lanes, glideways and testing.

  • In force · Standard

    Imperial Law Formatting Standard and Example

    The binding drafting format of every imperial legal document: A4 page and margin dimensions, Arial body text, the Title, Chapter, Article and subordinate-heading hierarchy, lists, tables, forms, prohibited formatting and a model code extract. The Tack, Restraint and Welfare Code is its controlling model, and it applies to an instrument even where that instrument is short, technical, administrative or stored outside the law archive — as this Standard itself is.

  • In force · Schedule

    Imperial Tack Measurement Schedule

    Measurement procedure and permitted values supporting the Tack, Restraint and Welfare Code.

Official templates

Forms issued by the Empire

  • In force · Template

    Imperial Working Equid Passport Template

    The identity document carried for every working equid.

  • In force · Template

    Imperial Carriage Equid Road and Work Certificate Template

    Certification of fitness for road and carriage work.

Reserved for drafting

Decided in principle, not yet written

  • Planned · Infrastructure

    Road authority and infrastructure law

    Construction, maintenance and control of the public road network, separated from the welfare codes that use it.

  • Planned · Livery

    Livery and service dress standard

    Design of service livery, currently held outside the welfare codes.

  • Planned · Registry

    Imperial Breeding and Registry Code

    Breeding practice and the imperial registry. A document of this title exists in the law archive, but it is a title with no text, so nothing is in force under it; this register previously listed it among the codes in force, which was wrong. The archive does not yet record what its content is to be.

  • Planned · Criminal law

    Imperial Criminal Code

    Reserved by the archive to define intent, attempt, conspiracy, corporate and command responsibility, evidentiary standards, jurisdiction, due process, appeals and severe non-lethal sentencing — among other things for horse slaughter, which is to be prohibited as the highest-grade equid-welfare criminal offense. Hrossagrið does not use the human death penalty.

  • Planned · Public health

    Addiction and public health codes

    Named by the archive alongside the criminal, customs, education and police codes as the instruments with which the root-cause prevention doctrine for alcohol, nicotine, smoking and recreational drug use must later be reconciled. The archive expressly leaves the required rehabilitation program and the evidentiary standard for state medicine open.

  • Planned · Medicines

    Pharmaceutical code

    Listed by the archive as a cross-code consequence still to be drafted: pharmaceutical approval and advertising law, controlled-medicine prescription tiers, emergency exceptions, and withdrawal and tapering standards.

  • Planned · Borders

    Customs code

    Border and customs offenses, still to be drafted, including the rules distinguishing prohibited consumer products from legitimate medical, scientific, industrial, veterinary and evidentiary materials carried under specific authorization.

  • Planned · Education

    Education code

    Reserved with the same group of codes. The archive also files here the redistribution of school, court, child-welfare, disability, occupational and forensic functions performed elsewhere by psychologists.

  • Planned · Policing

    Police code

    Reserved with the same group of codes. The archive records the substance of police conduct toward peaceful prohibited assemblies — identification of the violation, a clear dispersal order, minimum non-injurious contact, a duty to intervene, recorded and public accountability — but states that the exact penalties remain to be drafted.

  • Planned · Production standard

    Alcohol production standards

    Binding standards to define the permitted ingredients and composition of each alcoholic product while alcohol remains lawful during the gradual phase-out, so that a product made merely by diluting low-quality spirit with water does not qualify, with stated composition and a mandatory verifiable official regulatory mark on every bottle.

Note on this register. Titles and scope are drawn from the working corpus, and an instrument appears above as in force only where a document bearing that title exists in the law archive and carries text. Full texts are not yet published on this portal; they will be added instrument by instrument. The road-status standard is summarized in full under horses.
