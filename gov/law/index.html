The Imperial Legal Corpus

Because the state name itself remembers protected passage, equid welfare is not decorative symbolism: it follows directly from the Fifth Dynamic and sits near the centre of imperial law. The register below lists the instruments in force, the technical standards that support them, and the law still reserved for drafting.

Governing principle. In Hrossagrið, protecting a person while allowing their horse or other equid to be abused, seized, overworked, or used as leverage is not true protection. Every instrument below is read against that principle. For the doctrine it derives from, see the state and its doctrine.

Codes in force

Binding law. A code states obligations and offences; the standards that follow give the measurable detail.

  • In force · General code

    General Imperial Equid Welfare Code

    The principal welfare instrument of the Empire, covering equids in every setting rather than one line of work.

  • In force · Sectoral code

    Imperial Code for the Welfare of Working Carriage Equids

    Carriage welfare in full: passports, certification, workload and rest, weather burden, route safety, harness, headgear mounts, road-status inserts, lighting, ground-guidance lamps, emergency medical carriages, enforcement, offences and interim schedules.

  • In force · Sectoral code

    Imperial Tack, Restraint, and Welfare Code

    Equipment placed on the animal, and the limits on restraint. The Empire’s bitless-only welfare principle is stated here: where a horse can be controlled safely without a device in its mouth, human convenience does not justify the additional risk to the animal.

  • In force · Sectoral code

    Imperial Road and Driver Code

    Conduct on public roads and the duties of drivers, together with the universal requirement that every equid entering a public road displays its road status.

  • In force · Sectoral code

    Imperial Mounted Service and Road Patrol Code

    Binding law for mounted police, the mounted Road Guard, ridden couriers and patrol equids — ridden service, which is not carriage work and is therefore governed separately.

  • In force · Sectoral code

    Imperial Breeding and Registry Code

    Breeding practice and the imperial registry.

Technical standards

Standards carry the measurable detail: thresholds, dimensions, colours, testing. They are binding, and they are amended more often than the codes they serve.

  • In force · Standard

    Imperial Public-Road Equid Status Marking Standard

    The universal marking rule for every equid on a public road — ridden, driven, led, service, courier, freight, ceremonial or private. Status is shown by an interchangeable insert held in a marker-bearing frentera or muserola, road-grade retroreflective and self-illuminating in its assigned colour. No paint, dye, clipping, branding, glue or coat alteration is permitted.

  • In force · Standard

    Imperial Service Equid Selection and Breed Standard

    Selection for police, ambulance, taxi, courier, veterinary, fire, military and sovereign service. Endurance, recovery, road courage, temperament, traffic sanity and lawful bitless control decide fitness — not breed label, fashion or exaggerated type. Individual certification overrides breed label.

  • In force · Standard

    Imperial Equine Climatic Burden Standard

    Weather, humidity, wetness, cold, heat, wind, footing and suspension levels, expressed as burden thresholds.

  • In force · Standard

    Imperial Carriage Lighting and Signalling Standard

    Carriage lamps, emergency warnings, illuminated road-status markers and equid ground-guidance lamps.

  • In force · Standard

    Imperial Emergency Medical Carriage Technical Standard

    Ambulance speed, low jostle, suspension, emergency lanes, glideways and testing.

  • In force · Schedule

    Imperial Tack Measurement Schedule

    Measurement procedure and permitted values supporting the Tack, Restraint and Welfare Code.

Official templates

Forms issued by the Empire. Their layout is fixed by the instrument that creates them.

  • In force · Template

    Imperial Working Equid Passport Template

    The identity document carried for every working equid.

  • In force · Template

    Imperial Carriage Equid Road and Work Certificate Template

    Certification of fitness for road and carriage work.

Reserved for drafting

Law decided in principle but not yet written. Items are held here deliberately, so that carriage welfare law does not absorb road authority law, infrastructure law, breed policy or template content.

  • Planned · Infrastructure

    Road authority and infrastructure law

    Construction, maintenance and control of the public road network, separated from the welfare codes that use it.

  • Planned · Livery

    Livery and service dress standard

    Design of service livery, currently held outside the welfare codes.

Roads and the Hrossaway exception

The Empire fundamentally does not want ordinary motor roads. It also exists in the real world, and needs imports, exports, medicine, industrial equipment and raw materials. Refusing all motor freight on principle would damage the economy and create a great deal of new harm — a strange outcome for a system trying to minimise destruction.

Hrossaway is the resulting narrow exception: a closed motor-freight road running from an international port or border freight point to a specific transfer terminal. A truck enters, unloads, and the goods continue by rail or heavy horse-drawn freight teams. The principle is not abandoned; it is also not turned into a suicidal dogma.
