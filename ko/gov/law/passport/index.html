Imperial Working Equid Passport Template

The permanent individual record kept for every working equid in the Empire, from identification and custody to work, restriction, retirement, and death.

This Passport is the permanent individual welfare, identity, certification, work, health, restriction, and ownership record for a working equid. It does not replace specialised certificates. A certificate, licence, operator record, or owner declaration shall not override a stricter welfare restriction recorded here or imposed by law.

Status In force as the official Passport form. This page reproduces the blank template: the fields below are those the issuing authority, keeper, and veterinarian complete, and no section of it is reserved.

Section 11. Full rest-day record

A full rest day means no carriage work, riding, lunging, formal training, sport, public display, transport assignment, mounted service, active traction, or other labour. Ordinary care, turnout, compatible social activity, and voluntary low-demand movement remain permitted.

Brief movement, changing stands, passenger loading, vehicle changes, unhooking and rehitching, or moving between nearby operators does not reset a waiting, assigned-shift, work-block, daily, or rolling workload clock.

Section 13. Incident and welfare history

The template sets out three identical incident rows, each carrying:

  • Date
  • Incident
  • Veterinary assessment
  • Restriction or suspension
  • Review date
  • Authority verification

Mandatory reassessment triggers include illness, injury, lameness, collapse, heat or cold stress, respiratory distress, serious startle, runaway, collision, entanglement, harness injury, pregnancy change, foaling, lactation change, medication masking concern, route change, vehicle change, ownership change, service change, and material workload change.

Section 16. Revision and audit trail

No Passport entry shall be erased. Corrections shall preserve the original entry, identify the person making the change, state the reason, and carry a date and verification.

The template sets out three identical revision rows, each carrying:

  • Revision
  • Date
  • Sections changed
  • Previous record reference
  • Author
  • Authority verification

Section 17. Record retention and access

The Passport shall remain with the equid’s central record for life and for at least ten years after death. Ownership transfer, operator change, retirement, export, suspension, or revocation shall not destroy or restart the record.

The owner, keeper, operator, veterinarian, certifying authority, inspector, and competent welfare authority shall receive the access required by law. Falsifying, concealing, destroying, duplicating deceptively, or refusing lawful access to a Passport is an offence.

This Passport records lawful capacity; it does not guarantee that work is lawful on a particular day. Current welfare, climate, route, surface, medication, equipment, and recovery conditions shall always be reassessed. The stricter rule prevails.
